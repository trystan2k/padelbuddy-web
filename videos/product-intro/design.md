## Brand

- Product: `Padel Buddy`
- Personality: mobile-first, courtside, confident, practical, friendly
- Tone: polished sports utility, not hype-reel neon, not enterprise sterile

## Typography

- Display: `Outfit`
- Body: `Inter`
- Display weight: `900`
- Heading weight: `800`
- Strong weight: `700`
- Body weight: `500`
- Display tracking: `-1px`
- Body line-height: `1.35`

## Motion

- Energetic but controlled
- Fast setup beats, calm readable holds
- No glitches, no violent shakes, no esports neon
- Prefer blur-crossfade and push-slide transitions

## Composition

- Primary delivery format: Instagram-friendly portrait `1080x1920`
- Use stacked vertical zones and angled screenshot cards over centered web layouts
- Build around translucent panels on top of atmospheric motion
- Court references should be subtle: grid, score chips, serve markers, score logic cues
- Background texture required in every scene
- Accent color should stay visible at video scale

## Avoid

- Dark-only cyberpunk palette
- Generic SaaS gradients disconnected from brand
- Tiny web-sized text
- Dense paragraphs
- Heavy black shadows
