# Padel Buddy Product Intro

Style block:

- Brand colors: `#F4F0E7`, `#E9F3DF`, `#FCFAF6`, `#13233B`, `#5E6B7D`, `#2F7CF6`, `#1FA24A`, `#E28A1A`, `#D8EA42`, `#D7CFBF`, `#FFFFFF`
- Fonts: `Outfit` display, `Inter` body
- Mood: courtside calm, polished utility, mobile-first speed

Rhythm declaration:

- `slow-build-BUILD-breathe-CTA`

Global rules:

- 30 seconds total
- Background video runs full duration beneath every scene
- Subtle ambient music runs full duration beneath every scene
- Use blur-crossfade as primary transition
- Every scene gets layered texture: glow, grid, score cues, structural rules
- Every scene gets readable hero type and one secondary focal point
- Avoid hype-sports clichés and avoid dense UI screenshots

## Scene 1 — Hook / Title (0.0s-9.2s)

Concept:

- Viewer lands inside a soft cinematic court atmosphere. The product appears like a calm instrument already ready before first serve. The title fades in with confidence, not noise.

Mood direction:

- Clean premium sports utility. Editorial calm with athletic precision.

Depth layers:

- BG: moving light-canvas video wash, subtle court grid, soft radial glow
- MG: hero title, supporting subtitle, brand pill, score chip
- FG: top-left product icon, lower-right rally line, small metadata row

Animation choreography:

- Icon rises
- Brand pill slides
- Title fades and lifts in
- Subtitle cascades
- Score chip pops
- Accent rule draws across

Transition out:

- Blur crossfade, `0.55s`, `power2.inOut`

## Scene 2 — Feature World (8.9s-19.6s)

Concept:

- The piece opens into a tactical match-control board. Product value becomes concrete: score live, recover matches, work with remote and touch without slowing play.

Mood direction:

- Built-for-court tool. Productive, tactile, structured.

Depth layers:

- BG: same video wash with stronger blue-green glow, court lane dividers
- MG: three feature cards, scoreboard module, status chips, stat line
- FG: amber marker, serve indicator, slim dividers, floating label

Animation choreography:

- Headline glides in
- Cards cascade one by one
- Scoreboard panel stamps in
- Status chips snap on
- Stat line draws left-to-right

Transition out:

- Blur crossfade, `0.60s`, `power2.inOut`

## Scene 3 — Resolve / CTA (19.3s-30.0s)

Concept:

- End on readiness. The app feels installed, practical, and immediately usable. Viewer should leave with one memory: ready before first serve.

Mood direction:

- Warm resolve. Trustworthy, lightweight, action-ready.

Depth layers:

- BG: lighter video wash, wide glow, final highlight circle
- MG: CTA headline, installation/support badges, product promise, URL chip
- FG: small kicker label, closing divider, final wash for fade-out

Animation choreography:

- Kicker types on
- Headline sweeps in
- Promise line rises
- Store badges bounce lightly
- URL chip settles
- Final wash fades up while scene fades down

Transition out:

- Final fade only, `1.10s`, `power2.inOut`

Recurring motifs:

- Court-grid texture
- Blue and green accent pair
- Rounded panels with brand border color
- Score chips and serve markers as micro-details

Negative prompt:

- No glitch effects
- No neon magenta/cyan palette
- No fake 3D phone mockup
- No tiny text
- No crowded screenshot-led layout
